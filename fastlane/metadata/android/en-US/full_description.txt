Prism is an agent harness that runs on your computer. This app is its window on your phone: the same sessions, tools and settings, driven from the phone while the agent, its sandbox, its keys and its models stay on the computer.

The phone reaches the computer over the local network or Tailscale; there is no server in between. Pair the phone from Prism on the computer: Settings › Window › Link a phone in the window, or /phone in the terminal.
